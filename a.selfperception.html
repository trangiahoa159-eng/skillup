Self-Perception

🏛️ Core Pillars
🚀 Practical Action Framework
🔎

Evidence Check

When you notice a strong belief about yourself ("I'm bad at this"), test it against actual results or specific feedback before accepting it.

🧭

Personal Standards

Write down what "doing well" looks like for you, so you can judge your own work without waiting for someone else's approval.

📊

Then-vs-Now Comparison

Instead of comparing yourself to someone else's current stage, compare where you are now to where you were six months ago.

🗣️

Trusted Feedback Circle

Ask two or three people you trust for honest input, so your self-image reflects more than just your own perspective.

💡
Pro-Tip: If a belief about yourself hasn't been tested against real evidence recently, treat it as a hypothesis, not a fact.
