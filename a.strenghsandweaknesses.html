📋

Skills Inventory

List your top strengths and weaknesses honestly, then ask a trusted colleague or friend to add anything you might be missing.

🎯

One Weak Skill at a Time

Pick a single weak area and set a small, specific practice goal for it rather than trying to fix everything at once.

🔄

Feedback Loop

After finishing any task, ask someone directly: "What's one thing I could have done better here?"

📈

Progress Log

Track how a weak skill performs over time so you have real evidence of improvement, not just a feeling.

💡
Pro-Tip: A weakness you can name and are actively working on is far less limiting than a weakness you're pretending not to have.
