Values Identification

Identify the personal values that shape your decisions

🏛️ Core Pillars
🚀 Practical Action Framework
🃏

Values Card Sort

List 15-20 common values (honesty, freedom, family, achievement, creativity...) and sort them into "Most Important," "Somewhat Important," and "Least Important" piles until you narrow down your top 5.

✨

Peak Moments Journal

Write down 3-5 moments when you felt genuinely proud or fulfilled. The value present in each moment is usually a clue to what actually matters to you.

⚖️

Values Conflict Mapping

When two values pull you in different directions, write out what each choice protects, then decide based on the situation at hand rather than habit.

🔁

Quarterly Values Check-In

Revisit your top values every few months. New experiences and perspectives can reshape or refine what matters most, so treat your list as a living document.

💡
Pro-Tip: A value only counts once it's tested — the real signal is what you protect when convenience and principle pull in opposite directions.
