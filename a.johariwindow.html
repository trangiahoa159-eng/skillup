🏛️ Core Pillars
🚀 Practical Action Framework
🗨️

Ask for Direct Feedback

Regularly ask a trusted person a specific question, like "Is there anything about how I communicate that I might not notice?"

🔓

Appropriate Self-Disclosure

Share relevant, real information about yourself with people you trust, when the moment actually calls for it.

🧗

Try Something New

Step into an unfamiliar activity or situation \u2014 it's one of the few ways to surface traits from your Unknown Area.

🪟

Window Check-In

Every so often, reflect on all four areas together and notice which one has been neglected lately.

💡
Pro-Tip: The Open Area doesn't grow by accident \u2014 it grows every time you either disclose something real or actually listen to feedback.
