🏛️ Core Pillars
🚀 Practical Action Framework
📓

Weekly Journal Habit

Set aside a few minutes each week to write down what happened, how you reacted, and what you'd do differently.

❓

The 5 Whys

When something bothers you, ask "why" to your own answer five times in a row until you reach the real root cause.

🔍

Pattern Review

Every month, skim back through your notes looking for the same reaction or outcome showing up more than once.

🎯

One Lesson, One Action

For every reflection, land on exactly one concrete thing you'll try differently next time.

💡
Pro-Tip: Reflection that only replays the past without landing on an action is rumination, not growth \u2014 always end with one specific next step.
